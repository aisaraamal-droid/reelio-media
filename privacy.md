# Reelio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Reelio (Telegram bot @reelio_video_kz_bot and Instagram account @reelio_bot_kz) turns users' videos into short reels.

## What we collect
- Videos and messages you send to the Reelio bot, used only to make your reel.
- Public data of our own Instagram business account (posts, comments, insights), used to publish content and answer comments.

## How we use it
We use this data only to provide the service. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

## Storage
Videos are kept only as long as needed to deliver your reel and are then deleted.

## Data deletion
To delete your data, write "delete my data" to @reelio_video_kz_bot in Telegram or email ecovisakz@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.
